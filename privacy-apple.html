Privacy Policy — iOS App

2. Data we collect

The App only collects the data needed for it to work. All data listed below is linked to your account.

What the App does not collect: your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier (IDFA). The App contains no advertising or third-party analytics, does not track you for advertising purposes (as defined by App Tracking Transparency) and does not sell any data.

3. Biometrics and unlock

When you enable biometric unlock, the App uses native iOS APIs (Face ID / Touch ID, LocalAuthentication framework). Your biometric data (fingerprint, facial template) is never sent to Sesterce, never goes through our servers and is not stored by the App. It is handled exclusively by iOS, in your device's Secure Enclave. The App only receives a yes/no result of the verification. Enabling it is optional.

5. Retention

  • Account and profile data: kept for as long as your account is in use, then deleted or anonymized within 12 months after it is closed, unless a longer legal retention period applies.
  • Transactions, top-ups, payments and subscription: kept for the period required by law, in particular accounting and tax obligations.
  • Push notification token: kept while the App is installed and notifications are enabled; deleted when you revoke permission or delete your account.
  • Biometric data: not applicable — not collected or stored by Sesterce (see section 3).

6. Recipients and processors

Your data is neither sold nor rented to third parties. It may be shared only with the following recipients, strictly within the scope of their task:

  • Our hosting providers, for secure storage of account data;
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. and its affiliates), to process one-off payments (top-ups) and subscriptions. Stripe acts under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy;
  • Apple (Apple Push Notification service) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), for the technical delivery of push notifications to your iPhone;
  • The partner venue or merchant whose currency or card you use, to manage your account and your transactions with them;
  • Our technical service providers involved in maintenance, under a contractual confidentiality undertaking;
  • The competent authorities, where required by law.

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers (notably Stripe, Apple and Google) may process data outside the European Union. Where this happens, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards recognized by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions).

8. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data: encrypted communications (TLS), secure authentication, on-device storage in the iOS Keychain, hosting in a controlled environment, and access restricted to authorized personnel.

9. App Store “App Privacy” details

Consistent with this policy, the App Store “App Privacy” section states that the App collects data linked to your identity (contact information, identifiers, purchases) for app functionality, and that no data is used to track you.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Right of access to your data;
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data;
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”);
  • Right to restriction of processing;
  • Right to data portability;
  • Right to object to processing;
  • Right to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@sesterce.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL) or your local supervisory authority.

11. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time directly in the App: Profile tab, then “Delete my account” (“Supprimer mon compte”), then confirm. You can also make a request from this page or by writing to contact@sesterce.app (requests are handled within 30 days at most). Some data may be kept beyond that where the law requires it (e.g. accounting obligations).

12. Children

The App is not intended for people under 15. We do not knowingly collect data about children under 15 without the consent of their legal guardian.

13. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated, in particular to reflect changes to the App or to regulations. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page. In case of a material change, we will inform users through the App.

14. Contact

For any question about this privacy policy or the processing of your data: contact@sesterce.app.
